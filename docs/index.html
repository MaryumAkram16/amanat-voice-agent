What happens in one exchange

Five steps, running inside a single spoken sentence.

Real-time listening

Handles Urdu, Punjabi, and English mixed in the same sentence, transcribed as she speaks.

Structured extraction

Patient name, symptoms, and vitals pulled out precisely — never guessed. Missing details trigger one short follow-up.

Danger-sign triage

Every visit is screened against a reviewed red-flag list. Serious cases are flagged immediately, before anything else.
